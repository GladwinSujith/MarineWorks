Our Story

MarineWorks Pro was founded in 2004 by Captain Tom Reyes, a lifelong boater and certified Marine Technician who saw a gap in South Florida's repair market — shops that were too expensive, too slow, or too impersonal.

Tom started with one lift and two tool boxes. Today, MarineWorks operates from a 12,000 sq ft facility with five certified technicians and a reputation spanning Key Largo to Palm Beach.

Tom's son Marco joined in 2015 after completing his Mercury Marine Master Technician certification, carrying the same uncompromising standard forward.
